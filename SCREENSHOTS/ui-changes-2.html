PoRedoMedia UI changes: features brought back from the old apps

5 October 2026. Where a "before" exists it is on the left. Yellow numbers mark what is new. Captured from the app running in mock mode at 1440×900, so the pictures, titles and insults are placeholders.

Create: trim a video

Two sliders pick the stretch of the video to keep. Everything else in the run counts from the trimmed start.

  1. 1 Trim start and end, with the kept length shown. Shown for videos longer than 3 seconds.

Create: the run log

The lines the run reports are kept with the run, so they are still there after a reload.

  1. 1 Log: what was heard and seen, each cue the director placed (HIT), and each roast joke (ROAST).
  2. 2 Below the result (scrolled off here): "Download the roast alone (audio)".

Roast stage

The stage for a rap roast of a picture.

  1. 1 Each line is a button: it plays the track from where that line starts.
  2. 2 Lyric timing: moves the highlighting up to 3 seconds earlier or later.
  3. 3 Export video: records the picture, the current line and the track. Meme cut or Classic.
  4. 4 What the AI saw: the description the roast was written from.

Gallery: video exports

A video opened in the gallery.

  1. 1 GIF (first 8 seconds; making it uses one run) and Roast audio (the insults alone). An image gets Copy here instead.

Gallery: right-click menu

Right-click a card for its actions without opening it. With several items selected, Download now gives one ZIP.

  1. 1 Open, Use as source, Copy picture (images), Download, Pin, Delete.

Share: post to the feed

Sharing still makes a private link. The new switch also lists the item on the feed.

  1. 1 Off by default. On, every signed-in user can see the item on the Feed page.

Feed (new page)

What signed-in users have posted. The public share page now also shows a view count and, for Meme-ify videos, a remix link.

  1. 1 Feed in the main navigation.
  2. 2 Trending or New. Trending favours remixes, then views, and fades with age.
  3. 3 A post: player, who posted it, views and remixes. Remix re-times its sounds, captions and stickers onto your own video.
