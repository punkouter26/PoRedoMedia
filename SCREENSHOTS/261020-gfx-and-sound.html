GFX & Sound: G1–G10

Before and after, captured headless at 1366×860 with mock AI on 2026-10-20. Effects switch off by themselves for reduced motion, for automated browsers (the E2E tests) and from Settings → Visual effects. These shots force them on. Headless Chromium has no WebGPU device, so every shot here shows the WebGL2 path.

G2Gravity-well drop zone

Dragging a file over the picker: 56,000 GPU particles (WebGL2 transform feedback) launched into orbit around the pointer, spiralling in.

G1Liquid glass panels

The backdrop shader now draws real glass under every .po-glass panel (distance-field normals, lens refraction, chromatic fringes, pointer-lit rim, caustics).

G5Run reactor orb

A raymarched metaball orb above the steps: one blob per function, melted into the core as each step finishes.

G3Pixel-flow reveal

A new picture is rebuilt from the source, one particle per pixel, paired by brightness and hue, flying on curved spring paths (WebGPU compute when present, WebGL2 otherwise).

G8Confetti in the result's colours

k-means on a 32×32 copy of the result picks the palette; GPU-instanced pieces flip in 3D and flutter. Phones buzz.

G7Physics gallery

Deleting shatters the card into Voronoi shards with gravity and a bouncy floor. Cards tilt toward the pointer with a glint, can be thrown to delete, and picked cards lean together.

G92.5D preview while Veo works

Depth Anything V2 runs in a worker in the browser; a parallax-occlusion shader with depth-of-field dollies through the photo, steered by the pointer or the phone's tilt. Skipped under Save-Data.

G6Roast concert mode

While a roast plays, a screen opens over the lyrics: domain-warped plasma driven by pre-computed bass/mid/high bands, kick rings, chromatic punch, sparks, spring-physics letters, and a mic drop at the end.

The mock roast has no real track (its audio is a 404), so the probe played a library sound through the stage.

G10Living sound wall

Every sound gets a spectrogram ribbon (STFT in a worker, log-spaced rows, inferno heat ramp); click to play from that point; the playing one shows a glowing playhead and pulses with its loudness.

G4Sound engine + settings

Not visible in a screenshot: FM bells, a generated reverb, stereo placement by screen position, a pentatonic note per finished step, a working pad that opens with progress and ducks under roasts, plus shatter, shimmer, drop, whoosh and mic-drop sounds. Still no audio files.
